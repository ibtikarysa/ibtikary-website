Wndoo Sunshades

What is Wndoo?

Wndoo is an adjustable side window sunshade designed for vehicles, with a foldable design for easy storage.

Adjustable

Designed to adapt to different car window sizes.

Foldable

Easy to fold and store when not in use.

Developable

Model No. 1 provides a starting point for further product development.
