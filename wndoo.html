Wndoo Sunshades

What is Wndoo?

Wndoo is an adjustable side window sunshade designed for vehicles, with a foldable design for easy storage.

Adjustable

Designed to adapt to different car window sizes.

Foldable

Easy to fold and store when not in use.

Developable

Model No. 1 provides a starting point for further product development.

Why Wndoo?

Different sunshade designs solve the same problem in different ways. Wndoo is designed to address common challenges related to fit, installation, and adaptability.

Magnetic Sunshade

Magnetic attachment

Uses magnetic attachment to hold the sunshade around the vehicle window frame.

Metal Frame Sunshade

Vehicle-specific fit

Uses a fabric and metal frame design that requires a suitable size for the vehicle window.

Door-Top Sunshade

Door-mounted design

Extends over the upper part of the door to provide shade from both inside and outside.

Suction Cup Sunshade

Suction-cup attachment

Uses suction cups to attach the sunshade directly to the car window.
