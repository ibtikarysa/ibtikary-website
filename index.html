Frequently Asked Questions

What is Ibtikary?

Ibtikary is a commercial product licensing platform connecting manufacturers, licensors, and affiliate marketers through premium commercial licensing opportunities.

How does the licensing system work?

Each commercial license can only be sold once. Once a license is purchased, the next available license is offered at a higher price, increasing future affiliate commissions.

How much commission do affiliates earn?

Affiliates earn a 30% commission on every successful commercial licensing sale.

Is registration free?

Yes. Joining the Ibtikary Affiliate Program is completely free.

When do affiliates get paid?

Affiliate commissions are paid monthly via PayPal after qualifying sales have been verified.

Can anyone join the affiliate program?

Yes. Anyone interested in promoting commercial licensing opportunities can apply to become an affiliate.

What products can I promote?

You can currently promote Wndoo Sunshades, CarBeva, and KASHET commercial licensing opportunities.

How does the 30-day cookie work?

If someone clicks your affiliate link, you'll receive a commission if they complete a qualifying purchase within 30 days.
